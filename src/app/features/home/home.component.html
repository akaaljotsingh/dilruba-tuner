How to tune a wire

  1. Choose the wire in Wire Tuner and play it on its own.
  2. Wait for a stable pitch reading.
  3. Tighten if the needle is left (flat); loosen if it is right (sharp).
  4. Stop adjusting when the needle rests in the centre.

All listening happens on this device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, and the tuner works offline once installed.
